PokeDropper - art credits
=========================

The town, buildings, player and effects come from Tuxemon, a free and
open-source monster-catching game: https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon

Tilesets ("Core Outdoor", "Core Buildings", "Core Set Pieces") by rubberduck,
built on work by Buch (commissioned by luke83), George_, Kelvin Shadewing,
Midi, PastTheFuture, ArMM1998, Redshrike, Mike Bramson and other Tuxemon
contributors. Player sprite and animations by Tuxemon contributors; splash
based on "Fishing Splash" by Jesse McCarthy, adapted by Sanglorian.

The full list of every asset, author and licence:
https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon/blob/development/ATTRIBUTIONS.md

collection (https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites). Pokemon is (c) Nintendo,
Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK inc.; this is an unofficial fan project.
